# Word in Context Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Capture Word: pick a word from the selected sentence or paragraph and save it with its sentence and source.
- Browse Words: definitions from the Free Dictionary API, optional Raycast AI explanation of the word in its sentence.
- Casual Review: guess a handful of saved words from their blanked-out sentences, no schedule.
- Optional Markdown file that every captured word is appended to.
