# Word in Context

Save curious words **together with the sentence you read them in**, without leaving what you're reading.

## How to capture

1. Triple-click the sentence or paragraph containing the word (works in browsers, Mail, Slack, Notes, Word…).
2. Press your Capture Word hotkey.
3. Type two or three letters of the word and press Enter.

Raycast closes and shows "Saved: word". If you select just one word, it's saved immediately, without context.

**Tip:** give Capture Word a hotkey in Raycast Settings → Extensions, e.g. `⌥⇧W`.
With the [Raycast browser extension](https://www.raycast.com/browser-extension) installed, the page title and URL are saved as the source.

## Commands

- **Capture Word**: pick a word from the selection and save it with its sentence.
- **Browse Words**: every saved word with its sentence, dictionary definition and source. With Raycast Pro, *Explain in This Sentence* asks Raycast AI what the word means in that exact sentence, how formal it is, and gives a new example.
- **Casual Review**: about ten words at random (less-reviewed ones first) shown as blanked-out sentences. Reveal, move on. No streaks, no schedule.

## Preferences

- **Markdown File**: also append every captured word to a Markdown file (e.g. inside an Obsidian vault).
- **Minimum Word Length**: hide short words from the capture list.
- **AI Explanation**: explain words in context automatically when you browse them (Raycast Pro).

Definitions come from the [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/).
